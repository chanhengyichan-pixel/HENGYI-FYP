Draw room zonesFor each room camera: Edit → Zones, then mark the hazard and restricted areas.

Entrance inspection 0

Checks every person for a helmet and a safety vest at the door, then allows or denies entry. No zones.

Room monitoring 0

Watches a work area. Each room camera has its own zones: hazard (PPE required inside) and restricted (nobody may enter).
