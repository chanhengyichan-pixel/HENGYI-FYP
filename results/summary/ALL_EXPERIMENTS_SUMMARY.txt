CONCLUSION
──────────
YOLOv8 demonstrates the highest overall performance with 78.63% average mAP@0.5 and excellent
inference speed (78.74 FPS). It ranks 1st across most metrics and is recommended for production
deployment in industrial PPE compliance monitoring systems.

YOLOv11 provides the fastest inference (81.16 FPS average) with comparable accuracy (77.42% avg
mAP), making it suitable for real-time edge deployment with minimal latency.

YOLOv9 achieved the single highest peak score (93.32% on CHVG dataset) with good consistency
(6.32 pp range) but slightly lower average FPS (62.66) compared to YOLOv8 and YOLOv11.

All YOLO-family algorithms show strong performance (~77-79% avg mAP@0.5). SSD300 performs
significantly lower (35.13% avg mAP) but with stable FPS across datasets, suitable for
constrained edge deployment where speed is prioritized over accuracy.

Dataset Difficulty Analysis:
- CHVG (83.16% avg) — easiest dataset with balanced multi-class distribution
- Roboflow (71.17% avg) — small dataset with 5 classes, good baseline
- SHEL5K (66.36% avg) — largest test set (750 images), reliable evaluation
- SFCHD (65.44% avg) — 7-class chemical/hazardous domain
- SH17 (50.35% avg) — most challenging with 17 classes and complex scenarios

**Recommendation: YOLOv8** for production deployment due to best overall weighted score (0.9024)
combined with excellent accuracy (78.63% mAP) and inference speed (78.74 FPS).
